Find a Kindred Spirit

Whatever you love doing — studying, training, crafting, playing — find the right buddy or group who wants to do it too, nearby.

What's the problem? How will it function?

Free at launch · Now in development

The problem

Doing it alone is harder than it needs to be.

Students, athletes, and hobbyists all run into the same wall: wanting to do something, but not having the right person to do it with.

📚

Students

Studying solo is slower and harder to stick with.

"I need a study buddy to quiz me before my history test."
🏐

Athletes

Sports often need more than one person — and the right ones.

"I want people to practice volleyball at the beach with."
🎨

Hobbyists

Your hobby is more fun shared with people who get it.

"Looking for other painters for a weekend group session."

The solution

Match with the right buddy or group — then do the thing.

Find a Kindred Spirit connects you with people who want to do the same activity as you, at the same time, close enough to make it happen.

  • Post what you want to do and how many people you need
  • Get matched with others who share your interest
  • Meet up and enjoy the activity together

How it works

Three quick steps to your next hangout.

No endless swiping — just tell us what you want to do, and we'll find the people.

1

Tell us your interest

What do you want to do? Studying for a test, playing volleyball, hiking, painting — anything goes.

2

Set your group size

Looking for one study buddy or a whole team? Pick how many people you want to meet up with.

3

Choose your radius

Share your location and how far you're willing to travel, and we'll match you with kindred spirits nearby.
